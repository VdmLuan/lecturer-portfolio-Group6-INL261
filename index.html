Mr. Ikraam Sadek is a Junior Lecturer at Belgium Campus iTversity, where he teaches a wide range of modules spanning programming, web development, software analysis, and information systems.

He holds an Advanced Diploma in Application Development from Rosebank College — and he brings that practical, hands-on spirit into every class.

His teaching philosophy is simple: no one-sided lectures. Classes should feel like conversations. Real discussions. Debates about code. That's where the real learning happens.
